# 00 Project brief

Handover from the Lotus Feet Consulting Project (Claude project), dated 4 Nov 2026. Sarthak Chaturvedi is the project head and decides everything.

## Goal
A corporate website for Lotus Feet Consultancy (LF) that is found by search engines and cited by AI answer engines (SEO, AEO, GEO), earns a founder's trust, and sends them to a booked call with the CA.

## Reader
Founders and CXOs of startups and growing Indian businesses, looking for outsourced finance, a virtual or fractional CFO, compliance, valuation or funding help. Most will not know the brand name. They ask general questions, so the site answers general questions.

## Firm, as supplied by Sarthak
- Services: Virtual CFO, Valuation, Startup Consultancy, Business Funding, Pitch Deck and Financial Model. Compliance sits under these.
- LF is outside ICAI registration (confirmed by Sarthak, 2 Nov 2026). It is not a CA firm registered with ICAI.
- The CA's name is not used to market LF (confirmed by Sarthak, 2 Nov 2026).
- Call booking runs on Calendly. The CA takes the call.

## Scope of this repo
- Build: LF corporate site, Home first.
- Parked: Services page copy and per-service pages, About, Contact, legal pages. Order is Sarthak's call.
- Not here: the CA's personal portfolio.
- Also planned for the site (all need Sarthak's go-ahead per phase): blog, newsletter sign-up, SEO/AEO/GEO structure, privacy notice and terms drafts.

## Approval path
1. Sarthak builds with Claude Code.
2. Sarthak demos the CA in person and collects feedback before committing to anything.
3. The CA supplies real content: clients, case studies, key figures, testimonials, team details, official email, Calendly URL. Sarthak collects it from the CA on 4 Nov 2026.
4. Nothing goes public until Sarthak says so.
